## 📜 Protected Inventions & Subject Matter

The computational architecture, mathematical formulations, algorithmic logic, and software implementations contained within this repository represent proprietary inventions, including but not limited to:

1. **Structured State Memory (SSM)**: Methods for discrete positional state encoding and empirical Bayesian shrinkage modeling on combinatorial variant spaces.
2. **Pairwise Epistatic Residual Resonance (PERR)**: Methods for isolating, weighting, and summing second-order residue-pair interaction energies without structural models or continuous embeddings.
3. **Combinatorial Variant Generation & Ranking**: Algorithms for zero-shot prospective identification, ranking, and selection of high-fitness protein variants across high-dimensional sequence landscapes.
4. **Anti-Leakage Validation Protocols**: Deterministic hashing, preregistration protocols, and cryptographic candidate freeze workflows.
